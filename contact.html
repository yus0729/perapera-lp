What to include in your message

  • Your account email (so we can locate your data)
  • Device model and OS version (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17.5)
  • App version (visible at the bottom of the My Page screen)
  • A clear description of the issue or request
  • Screenshots, if applicable

Response time

We reply within 2 business days (Japan time). For urgent billing or refund issues, please contact Apple or Google Play directly, as we cannot process refunds on our side.
